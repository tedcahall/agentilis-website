Case Studies

Welcome Videos, Product Explainers and a LinkedIn Series, Produced by an AI Agent Team

A finished business video usually takes a crew: a writer, an illustrator, a voice artist, an animator and an editor. In our own operations, an agent team does that work, including two welcome videos made as sales examples to show prospective clients. It writes the script and shot list, records the narration with an AI voice, draws and animates every shot, cuts the picture to the voice, builds the captions, and encodes the files for the website and social media. A person approves the script, the budget and every cut before anything is published.

What the Agents Do
  1. Write the script and the shot list, timed at 150 words a minute, for a person to approve
  2. Lock a style guide: color palette, characters and camera rules, with character sheets approved before any shot is made
  3. Record the narration with an approved AI voice, or edit the owner's own phone recordings and voice takes
  4. Draw a keyframe for every shot with an image model, at about $0.14 each, and check every figure at full size
  5. Animate the keyframes into 1080p video clips, and build screens, charts and every on-screen word in code
  6. Cut the picture to the voice and add music
  7. Time the captions from the finished cut's audio, using the approved script word for word
  8. Review contact sheets and frames, count the arms, hands and legs on every figure, and regenerate any shot that fails
  9. Encode the deliverables: a full-quality master, a smaller web copy, and horizontal and vertical versions for social media
The Hard Cases
  • Exact words on screen: no words are generated inside AI images. Every label, name and number on screen is added in code and spell-checked, so on-screen text is always exactly right.
  • A quality bar on every shot: every shot goes through quality review before it is used. A shot is regenerated until it passes, and when a shot calls for a different approach, it is rebuilt that way. Only shots that pass make the cut.
  • Voice timing: the AI voice's word timings can run up to 75 milliseconds late, so the narration is only ever cut in the middle of a silence.
  • Phones: web videos ship as MP4, the format that plays everywhere, including Safari on iPhone.
Guardrail

A person approves the script, the voice, the look and the budget before production starts. Budgets are approved section by section, for example up to $10 for the opening and up to $15 for a later section, and image work stops at the approved cap. Every cut goes to a marketing agent for review first and then to a person, and nothing is published without that person's approval. Sales examples carry no real client logos, and every number on screen is marked as a sample.
