Company Intelligence Reports in Minutes for a Management Consulting Firm

ChangeBeat, a European management consulting firm, often spent days researching a single US company in its ideal customer profile. They asked whether there was a faster way. In an early Agentilis project, a research pipeline was built that turns a company name and website into an analyst-grade intelligence report in about five minutes, delivered as PDF, Word and PowerPoint.

Where the Data Comes From
  • The company website: products, pricing, leadership and brand
  • SEC EDGAR: 10-K and 10-Q filings, proxy statements with executive pay, and Form D funding filings
  • Financial Modeling Prep: three years of financials, ratios, analyst consensus, earnings calls and stock news
  • Apollo.io: the current leadership team
  • Wikipedia and Wikidata: founders, funding and history for private companies
  • Google News: recent coverage of the company
  • Google Patents, via BigQuery: more than 90 million patents, with Lens.org as a backup
  • Crunchbase: private-company funding, where available
What the System Does
  1. Takes a company name and website
  2. Determines whether the company is public or private, which decides where to look
  3. Gathers the facts with deterministic code, no AI: financials, filings, leadership, funding, news and patents
  4. Packages everything into one structured JSON file
  5. Claude reads the JSON, does its own web research, and writes the analysis: executive summary, competitors, risks and opportunities
  6. Claude returns its analysis as JSON in a fixed structure
  7. A deterministic formatter turns that JSON into the requested formats: PDF, Word, PowerPoint or Markdown
Guardrail

AI is used only where judgment is needed. The facts are gathered by deterministic code, and the reports are formatted by deterministic code. The raw data and the AI analysis are saved with every report, and every report lists its sources, so any finding can be traced back to where it came from.

The Result

ChangeBeat reported that the reports saved hours of research work for their US clients.

What It Means for You

What research does your team still do by hand?

Research that draws on the same sources every time can be automated. Deterministic code gathers the facts, AI does the thinking, and deterministic code formats the result. Each part does what it does best.
