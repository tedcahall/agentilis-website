Case Studies

Live Race Results at the Track for the Washington DC Region SCCA

On race weekends, results from every qualifying session, heat and feature race had to be pulled from the live timing system, matched to the right run group, loaded and published while racing was still going on. 24 to 27 times a weekend depending on the number of run groups that weekend. By hand, at the track, while racing continued. Now an agent team does all of it, with no human in the loop.

What the Agents Do
  1. Ken's monitor checks the live timing system every 5 minutes, Saturday and Sunday
  2. When a new session appears, it wakes Inman
  3. Inman waits until that group is off the track and the next group is live
  4. Skips sessions already loaded and races that don't count for points
  5. Pulls the results and matches them to the right run group and race
  6. Merges them into the points database, matching every driver by SCCA number
  7. Rebuilds the class standings on marrspoints.com
  8. Confirms the results landed, retries if they didn't, and texts the system owner a summary
Guardrail

A group is never processed while it is still on track. Timing systems sometimes release early, partial results, and publishing those is worse than waiting five minutes. Live results are marked provisional. The official results follow after the weekend through a separate, fully automated path. Nothing fails silently: if a step fails, it retries, and the system owner is notified.
