The Situation

The harder an agent works, the more leads go cold.

Real estate leads arrive by email from Zillow, Homes.com and other sites, usually while the agent is out doing the job: at a showing, in the car, at a closing. Industry studies put the average agent's response time to a new lead at 11 hours, and find that 78 percent of buyers choose the first agent who responds.

Brokerages have a second problem on top of the first. With agents spread across showings and closings, getting each lead to the right agent quickly is a distribution job, and most brokerages still do it by hand.

Rules vs. Judgment

Fast where it can be exact. Careful where it can't.

Rules: exact, every time
  • Match a lead email by sender, subject and body
  • Reply with the agent's own template, phone number and booking link
  • Text the agent the lead's number right away
  • Route by rotation, by weight, or to the home's listing agent
Judgment: where a person stays in the loop
  • Tell a real lead from a newsletter, receipt or duplicate
  • Read lead emails that don't follow the usual format
  • Send anything it can't read cleanly to a Needs Review queue for a person
What Was Solved

A launch-ready product, without the usual first hires.

  1. A product built for agents in the field.
    Four steps a busy agent understands: monitor the inbox, detect a real lead, respond in the agent's name, and connect by text. Brokers get lead routing, listing-agent rules and company-wide templates.
  2. A production application.
    Gmail inbox monitoring, lead matching and extraction, text and email outreach, an operations console, and separate portals for brokers and agents.
  3. Verified reliability.
    Simulated leads from each listing site are run end to end, delivery of every text template is confirmed at every level, and each defect is tracked to resolution.
  4. A market-ready launch.
    A complete marketing site and an 81-second explainer video, with script, voice, illustration, animation and captions.
