In Detail

How each service works.

Executive AI Advisory

Scheduled one-on-one sessions, weekly, biweekly, or monthly, that build your personal command of AI.

AI changes every week. Your board, your team, and your competitors all have opinions about it. We help you form your own. Each session covers what changed, what it means for your business, and what you should do about it.

You bring the questions. The vendor pitch on your desk. The article your board chair forwarded. The project that is over budget. You leave with a clear point of view and the confidence to lead the conversation.

What you get
  • A standing session on your calendar, at the cadence you choose
  • A short briefing on what changed in AI since last time, and why it matters to you
  • A sounding board for strategy, vendors, budgets, and hiring

Best for: CEOs and senior executives who want a trusted voice in the room before committing to an AI direction.

AI Discovery

A prepared working session with your leadership team that finds and scopes your three best AI programs.

Most companies do not lack AI ideas. They lack a way to choose. Before we meet, we research your business, send your CEO a questionnaire, and talk with two or three of your leaders. We arrive with a list of 10 to 15 ideas.

Then we spend half a day with your team. We map where the business loses time and money, score every idea, and put the top three through hard questions. You finish with one shared direction instead of a dozen competing ones.

What you get
  • Three scoping documents: the problem, metric, approach, data needs, guardrails, timeline, and owner
  • A ranked backlog of the ideas you did not pick
  • A walkthrough of the results with your leadership team

Best for: Leadership teams with plenty of AI ideas and no agreement on which ones to fund first.

AI Process Engineering

Redesigning specific workflows end to end so AI removes cost and delay.

AI does not have to reach every department to deliver company-wide results. We pick the workflows that matter most and map how the work really happens today: the handoffs, approval chains, data entry loops, and reconciliation cycles that eat your team's time.

Then we redesign the process around agents and people working together. Sales ops. Finance. HR. Customer success. Legal review. We define what the right output looks like, so you can tell when the new process is working.

What you get
  • End-to-end maps of your critical workflows
  • A before and after model of cost and cycle time
  • A redesigned process, ready to build and pilot in one department

Best for: Operations leaders who know where the friction is and want to prove the model in one area before scaling.

AI Program Development

Turning a scoped idea into a funded program with an owner, a plan, and a measure of success.

A good idea is not a program. A program has a business case, an owner, a budget, the right data, and rules for where AI may act alone and where a person decides. We build that structure with you, so the work survives past the pilot.

We also help your leaders bring the organization along. We reshape roles, set governance, and plan the rollout. AI that people resist does not deliver results.

What you get
  • A business case with the KPIs that define success
  • A phased roadmap covering data, tools, vendors, people, and budget
  • Operating model, governance, and change leadership

Best for: Mid-market and enterprise organizations ready to go beyond pilots and make AI part of how they operate.

Agentic AI Projects

Design and delivery of agent solutions on OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, and other frameworks, in the cloud or on your own servers.

Your front office should never sleep, never forget, and never drop a lead. Your back office is where margin lives. Agents can do the work in both, around the clock, and scale without adding headcount.

We design the agents, connect them to your systems, test them, and keep them running. They escalate to a human when judgment is needed. When data should never leave the building, we select, price, and set up private models on hardware you own.

Where agents work
  • Front office: inquiries, lead qualification, scheduling, and follow-up
  • Back office: reporting, approvals, vendor communications, invoices, and reconciliation
  • Go-to-market: prospect research, personalized outreach, and pipeline follow-through
  • Private and local: open-source models on your own servers, so sensitive data stays in-house

Best for: Companies ready to move from a pilot to agents that run in production every day, including those in regulated industries.

AI KPI Monitoring and Management

Tracking what your AI programs deliver, and managing them so the results hold up.

Launch day is not the finish line. Models change, costs drift, and a process that worked in March can quietly stop working in June. We define the KPIs for each AI program, set the baseline, and track performance against it.

Then we manage what the numbers show. We tune agents and prompts, adjust model choices to control cost, review the exceptions that went to a person, and recommend what to expand, fix, or stop.

What you get
  • A KPI scorecard for each program: business results, quality, cost, and adoption
  • Monitoring and alerts when an agent falls outside its limits
  • A regular performance review with clear recommendations

Best for: Companies with AI in production that want proof it is paying off and someone accountable for keeping it that way.
