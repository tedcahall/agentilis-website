The Problem

You've tried AI. You're still waiting for the payoff.

Licenses
The licenses went out. The work stayed the same.

Everyone got access to an AI assistant. A few people use it well. Most of the company works exactly as it did before.

Pilots
The pilot impressed the room, then stalled.

Nobody owned the move from proof of concept to daily operations, so the business never saw the benefit.

Vendors
The vendor closed the tickets. The business didn't change.

You got what the statement of work promised: new systems, delivered on schedule. How the company runs, and what it costs to run, stayed the same.

Spend
The AI budget grew. No one can say what it bought.

You see the invoices but not the results. When the board asks what the AI spend produced, there is no clear answer.

Agentilis starts from the other end: the business result you need first, then the AI that delivers it, from people who run their own company on AI every day.
