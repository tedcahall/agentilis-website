Services: Automate

Agentic AI Projects

Design and delivery of agent solutions on OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, and other frameworks, in the cloud or on your own servers.

Your front office could work around the clock and follow up on every lead. Your back office is where margin lives. Agents can do the work in both, and could help you grow without adding headcount.

We design the agents, connect them to your systems, test them, and keep them running. They can escalate to a human when judgment is needed. When data should never leave the building, we select, price, and set up local AI models on hardware you own.

Best for: Companies ready to move from a pilot to agents that run in production every day, including those in regulated industries.

Where agents can work
  • Front office: inquiries, lead qualification, scheduling, and follow-up
  • Back office: reporting, approvals, vendor emails, invoices, and reconciliation
  • Marketing: prospect research, personalized outreach, and pipeline follow-up
  • Local AI: open-source models on your servers, so sensitive data stays in-house

What agents can do

Sales

Agents can research prospects, qualify inbound leads, and draft follow-up. You could ask for a report on a prospect and have an agent run it and deliver it.

  • A consulting firm: days of research, now about five minutes →
  • Our own operations: a 25-page report in 4 to 6 minutes →

Marketing

Agents can research, draft, and produce content, from newsletters to finished video. A person can approve what gets published.

  • Our own operations: a first full video cut in a day →

Customer service

Agents can answer routine questions, look up information, and draft replies to harder ones. Requests that need judgment can go to a person, with the history attached.

Finance

Agents can gather receipts and invoices, file and reconcile them, and enter them in the books. Mismatches can go to a person for review.

  • A software foundation: 638 charges filed and recorded →

Operations

Agents can watch for incoming work, process it, and confirm the result. They can also draft plans and schedules from your rules, and a person can make the final call.

  • SCCA: hours of results processing, cut to minutes →
  • SCCA: race results with zero hours of staff time →
  • Jazzercise NRV: AI picks the songs, the instructor decides →

HR

Agents can answer policy questions, help schedule interviews, and guide new hires through onboarding. Hiring decisions can stay with people.

IT and software

Agents can modernize older systems, test changes, and build new products from a written spec. People can set the direction and review the work.

  • SCCA: a decade-old system modernized, zero downtime →
  • Real estate: from written spec to a live server in 3 hours →

Legal and compliance

Agents can sort contracts, point out unusual terms, and gather evidence for an assessment. Legal decisions can stay with your counsel.

  • Real estate: security assessment passed the first time →
