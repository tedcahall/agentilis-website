Services: Advise

AI Discovery

A Discovery sized to your company, to find your AI opportunities and decide where to start.

Most leadership teams have tried a chatbot. Few have seen an agent at work, or know how it differs from an AI automation. An automation follows steps set in advance, with AI handling some of them. An agent gets a goal, works out the steps, and brings in a person when judgment is needed. We start by making that clear, using your own business.

No two companies need the same Discovery. Yours might cover one team and one process, or the whole company. So before we propose one, we hold a free one-hour Discovery assessment to understand the opportunity and how big your Discovery should be.

Best for: Leadership teams that know AI will be a strategic advantage in their business and want to see which opportunities to prioritize before they commit to building anything.

How it works
  • Discovery assessment, one hour, free: we learn about your business and size the discovery engagement deliverable
  • Your proposal: a written scope and price, based on what we learned
  • Your Discovery: the work you approve, ending with your best opportunities and where to start

Where your opportunities may be

Sales

Opportunities could be in lead response, prospect research, proposals, or follow-up. A Discovery can show where deals slow down and which fix may be worth the most.

Marketing

Opportunities could be in content, campaigns, or reporting. A Discovery can show which work repeats every week and what your team could do with the time.

Customer service

Opportunities could be in the requests that arrive most often, or the ones that take longest. Each may call for a different answer.

Finance

Opportunities could be in invoices, receipts, reconciliation, or the monthly close. A Discovery can show what might be checked as it arrives instead of at month end.

Operations

Opportunities could be in work that waits on one person, one inbox, or one spreadsheet. These may be the clearest places to start.

HR

Opportunities could be in hiring, onboarding, or policy questions that come up again and again. A Discovery can also note which decisions should stay with people.

IT and software

Opportunities could be in older systems, testing, support tickets, or data access. A Discovery can show which may be ready now and which might need groundwork first.

Legal and compliance

Opportunities could be in contract review, policy updates, or assessment requests. A Discovery can also raise the rules that may limit where AI is used.
