AI KPI Monitoring and Management

Tracking what your AI programs deliver, and managing them so the results can hold up.

Launch day is not the finish line. AI models change, costs drift, and a process that worked in March can quietly stop working in June. We define the KPIs for each AI program, set the baseline, and track performance against it.

Then we manage what the numbers show. We adjust how the AI works, control its cost, review the exceptions that went to a person, and recommend what to expand, fix, or stop.

Best for: Companies with AI in production that want proof it is paying off and someone accountable for keeping it that way.

What you get
  • A KPI scorecard for each program: business results, quality, cost, and adoption
  • Monitoring and alerts when an agent falls outside its limits
  • A regular performance review with clear recommendations

What we can track

Sales

We can track response time, follow-up completed, and meetings booked. If lead quality slips, we can look into why and adjust the agent.

Marketing

We can track output, approval rate, and cost per finished piece. If more drafts come back for edits, we can adjust the agent's instructions.

Customer service

We can track resolution rate, time to answer, satisfaction, and how often a person steps in. Each handoff may show what to fix next.

Finance

We can track documents processed, exceptions, mismatches, and cost per transaction. Alerts can go out when a number leaves its limits.

Operations

We can track cycle time, volume, error rate, and hours returned to the team. Results can be compared with a baseline set before launch.

HR

We can track time to schedule, onboarding steps completed, and questions answered. Reviews can also check that people are making the decisions reserved for them.

IT and software

We can track uptime, AI cost, failed runs, and test results. When an AI model changes or a cheaper one might do, we can test it before switching.

Legal and compliance

We can track reviews completed, items escalated, and agent actions outside the rules. A record can be kept, so you can answer when someone asks.
