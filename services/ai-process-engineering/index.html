Services: Accelerate

AI Process Engineering

Redesigning specific workflows end to end so AI can remove cost and delay.

AI does not have to reach every department to deliver company-wide results. We pick the workflows that matter most and map how the work really happens today: the handoffs, approval chains, data entry loops, and reconciliation cycles that eat your team's time.

Then we redesign the process around agents and people working together. Sales ops. Finance. HR. Customer success. Legal review. We define what the right output looks like, so you can tell when the new process is working.

Best for: Operations leaders who know where the friction is and want to prove the model in one area before scaling.

What you get
  • End-to-end maps of your critical workflows
  • A before and after model of cost and cycle time
  • A redesigned process, ready to build and pilot in one department

Processes we can redesign

Sales

We can map the path from first inquiry to signed deal and look for the research, data entry, and handoffs that may slow it down. Agents could take on the preparation, so people can do the selling.

  • A consulting firm: days of research, now about five minutes →

Marketing

We can map how a campaign moves from idea to approval to launch, and where drafts may wait. Agents could take on repeat work, and people can keep the approvals that matter.

Customer service

We can trace a request from the first message to the resolution and look for repeat questions, transfers, and lookups. Routine cases could move to agents, and harder ones could reach the right person sooner.

Finance

We can follow invoices, receipts, and approvals from arrival to the books. A redesigned path could match and check documents as they arrive.

  • A software foundation: 638 charges filed and recorded →

Operations

We can look for work that waits on one person, one inbox, or one spreadsheet. Routine steps could run on their own, and exceptions could go to a person.

  • SCCA: hours of results processing, cut to minutes →
  • SCCA: race results with zero hours of staff time →
  • Jazzercise NRV: 15 hours a week of hand planning, redesigned →

HR

We can map hiring and onboarding, including the forms and reminders nobody owns. Agents could keep the steps moving, and managers can make the decisions.

IT and software

We can map how requests, changes, and releases move through the team. Agents could take on triage, testing, and documentation, and people can approve what ships.

Legal and compliance

We can map how contracts and policies get reviewed, and separate the routine from the risky. Agents could do a first read, so counsel can focus where the risk is.
