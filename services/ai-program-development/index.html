AI Program Development

Turning a scoped idea into a funded program with an owner, a plan, and a measure of success.

A good idea is not a program. A program has a business case, an owner, a budget, the right data, and rules for where AI may act alone and where a person decides. We build that structure with you, so the work can survive past the pilot.

We also help your leaders bring the organization along. We reshape roles, set governance, and plan the rollout. AI that people resist does not deliver results.

Best for: Mid-market and enterprise organizations ready to go beyond pilots and make AI part of how they operate.

What you get
  • A business case with the KPIs that define success
  • A phased roadmap covering data, tools, vendors, people, and budget
  • Operating model, governance, and change leadership

What a program can cover

Sales

A program can start with one owner and one number to move, such as response time or win rate. The plan can cover your CRM data, the tools, and how reps might use them.

Marketing

A program can set the business case, the budget, and the brand rules up front. It can say what AI may publish on its own and what a person approves.

Customer service

A program can define which requests AI may resolve alone and which go to a person. Targets for speed, quality, and satisfaction can be set before rollout.

Finance

We can build the business case with your finance team, along with the controls. Those could include approvals, an audit trail, and limits on what an agent may post.

Operations

A program can name a first process, give it an owner, and phase the rest. Each project can carry a status, a next step, and a person who sets the priority.

HR

A program can include new roles, training, and clear communication. It can also set where AI may help with hiring and where it may not decide.

IT and software

A program can settle the data access, security, and vendor choices it depends on. It can also plan how AI agents might remember past decisions and your rules as the work grows.

Legal and compliance

We can help you draft governance for your counsel to review. It could cover acceptable use, data handling, and human review.
