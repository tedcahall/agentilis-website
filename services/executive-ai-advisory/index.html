Services: Advise

Executive AI Advisory

Scheduled one-on-one sessions, weekly, biweekly, or monthly, that build your personal command of AI.

AI changes every week. Your board, your team, and your competitors all have opinions about it. We help you form your own. Each session covers what changed, what it means for your business, and what you should do about it.

You bring the questions. The vendor pitch on your desk. The article your board chair forwarded. The project that is over budget. You can leave with a clear point of view and the confidence to lead the conversation.

Best for: CEOs and senior executives who want a trusted voice in the room before committing to an AI direction.

What you get
  • A standing session on your calendar, at the cadence you choose
  • A short briefing on what changed in AI since last time, and why it matters to you
  • A sounding board for strategy, vendors, budgets, and hiring

Questions you can bring

Sales

Which AI sales tools are worth buying, and which features might your CRM already include? We can help you tell a real gain in pipeline from a good demo.

Marketing

How much of your content should AI write, and what could it do to your brand and your search traffic? We can help you set a policy you can defend.

Customer service

Which conversations could AI handle, and when should a customer reach a person? We can talk through the cost, the risk, and what your customers may accept.

Finance

What should you spend on AI this year, and how will you know if it paid off? We can help you weigh building against buying and question the business case.

Operations

Where might AI be ready to do the work, and where is it still a pilot? We can help you choose a first process and set expectations for it.

HR

Which roles could change, and what should you tell your people? We can help you think through hiring, training, and the message.

IT and software

Should your data stay in-house, and which AI products and vendors should you consider? You can hear from people who run AI every day, in the cloud and on their own equipment.

Legal and compliance

What could your exposure be when employees use AI on their own? We can help you frame the questions for counsel.
