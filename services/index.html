Advise

The most important AI decisions your company will make will be made in the boardroom, not the server room.

We give senior leaders a trusted, experienced voice on AI with no hype and no jargon. Just straight answers from people who build and run it.

Executive AI Advisory

Scheduled one-on-one sessions, weekly, biweekly, or monthly, that build your personal command of AI.

AI changes every week. Your board, your team, and your competitors all have opinions about it. We help you form your own. Each session covers what changed, what it means for your business, and what you should do about it.

You bring the questions. The vendor pitch on your desk. The article your board chair forwarded. The project that is over budget. You leave with a clear point of view and the confidence to lead the conversation.

What you get
  • A standing session on your calendar, at the cadence you choose
  • A short briefing on what changed in AI since last time, and why it matters to you
  • A sounding board for strategy, vendors, budgets, and hiring

Best for: CEOs and senior executives who want a trusted voice in the room before committing to an AI direction.

AI On Call

Direct access to a team that works in AI every day, for urgent questions and special projects.

Some questions cannot wait for the next scheduled meeting. When a deal depends on an AI claim, a project hits a wall, or a competitor announces something that sounds alarming, you get a fast answer from people who run agents in production.

For bigger questions, we take on special projects: model and vendor selection, due diligence on an AI acquisition or partner, or a second opinion on a plan before you fund it.

What you get
  • A direct line to our team for urgent AI questions
  • Second opinions on vendor proposals and AI plans
  • Special projects, scoped and priced as they come up

Best for: Leadership teams that need AI expertise on call without hiring a full-time AI executive.

In practice: ongoing advisory calls with executives at a real estate hardware & software firm and a national hospitality company.
Transform

Most companies do not need more software. They need to rethink how work gets done.

Buying AI tools is easy. Changing how a company works is the hard part. We have led organizations through big technology shifts before. This one is bigger.

AI Transformation

Leading your organization, people, and operating model through the shift to AI.

It starts with an honest readiness assessment: your data, your processes, your people, and the specific places where AI can deliver the fastest, highest-value results. You get a prioritized roadmap, not a theoretical framework.

Then we help you execute it. We set the governance, reshape roles and teams, and help your leaders bring the organization along. AI that people resist does not deliver results.

What you get
  • AI Readiness Assessment across data, process, and people
  • A prioritized roadmap tied to business results
  • Operating model, governance, and change leadership

Best for: Mid-market and enterprise organizations ready to go beyond pilots and make AI structural.

AI Process Re-Engineering

Redesigning specific workflows end to end so AI removes cost and delay.

AI transformation does not have to happen company-wide to deliver company-wide results. We pick the workflows that matter most and map how the work really happens today: the handoffs, approval chains, data entry loops, and reconciliation cycles that eat your team's time.

Then we redesign the process around agents and people working together. Sales ops. Finance. HR. Customer success. Legal review. Wherever the bottleneck is, we find it and fix it.

What you get
  • End-to-end maps of your critical workflows
  • A before and after model of cost and cycle time
  • A redesigned process, ready to build and pilot in one department

Best for: Operations leaders who know where the friction is and want to prove the model in one area before scaling.

In practice: for the Washington DC Region of the SCCA, we refactored a points scoring system with agentic coding and automated race weekend processing of live results through agentic workflows.
Build

Agentic AI is not a chatbot. It is a team that does the work.

We do not hand you a slide deck and leave. We build it, run it, and stand behind it. We know how because our own company runs on it.

Agentic AI Projects

Design and delivery of agent solutions on OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, and other frameworks.

Your front office should never sleep, never forget, and never drop a lead. Your back office is where margin lives. Agents can do the work in both, around the clock, and scale without adding headcount.

We design the agents, connect them to your systems, test them, and keep them running. They escalate to a human when judgment is needed.

Where agents work
  • Front office: inquiries, lead qualification, scheduling, and follow-up
  • Back office: reporting, approvals, vendor communications, invoices, and reconciliation
  • Go-to-market: prospect research, personalized outreach, and pipeline follow-through

Best for: Companies ready to move from a pilot to agents that run in production every day.

Private and Local AI

Hardware selection, pricing, and setup of AI models that run on your servers. Sensitive data stays in-house.

Some data should never leave the building. Open-source models have closed much of the gap with frontier models, and they run on hardware you own, at a fraction of the cost of public APIs.

We help you decide which workloads belong on your own hardware and which belong in the cloud. Then we size it, price it, and set it up. We run large open-source models on our own hardware every day, so we know what works and what does not.

What you get
  • A workload review: what runs locally and what stays in the cloud
  • Hardware selection and pricing
  • Setup, model deployment, and hybrid routing between private and frontier models

Best for: Healthcare, finance, legal, government, and other regulated industries, plus any company whose data is too sensitive for a public AI service. Lower API costs come as a bonus.

In practice: We run three different local models on Nvidia DGX Spark clusters and Mac Studio servers. We can help run dedicated cloud instances or local AI on consumer-grade hardware. Our agents handle research, newsletters, scheduling, billing, software development, and cloud operations.
